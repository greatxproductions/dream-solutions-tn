## Accurate facts you may use (verified, EPA)
- Radon is a naturally occurring, colorless, odorless, radioactive gas.
- Radon is the second leading cause of lung cancer overall and the leading cause among non-smokers (EPA).
- The EPA action level is 4.0 pCi/L — at or above this, EPA recommends mitigation.
- The only way to know a home's radon level is to test; levels vary from home to home, even next door.
